## What the slice takes from them
- Divided carriageway, 3 lanes each way, white 3 m dashes, white kerb-side edge line.
- Raised median with low clipped hedge, coconut palms and **twin-arm** street lights on the median.
- Kerbs painted in alternating black/white blocks; footpaths of red or grey interlocking pavers.
- Wide-canopy rain trees on the verge; compound walls in front of IT campuses; bus shelters at bus bays.
- Hazy, bright, slightly warm daylight; green overhead direction gantries.
